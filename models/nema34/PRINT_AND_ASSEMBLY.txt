NEMA 34 STALL LEVER — 34HS31-6004S1 — millimetres

SHAFT INTERFACE
This is a round keyed shaft, not a D shaft. A separate steel key bridges the
shaft groove and the matching groove in the lever. Do not use a printed key.
Shaft: nominal diameter 14.0. Bore: 14.30 (0.15 radial clearance).
Steel key: 5 x 5 x 20 recommended. Hub groove: 5.20 wide, top 9.20 from axis.
The reference drawing shows 5 mm key width and 16 mm from the opposite edge
of the 14 mm shaft to the top of the key, giving a key top 9 mm from axis.
The 5 mm key height is the selected standard hardware size; verify it seats
in your motor. Nominal shaft groove floor is consequently 4 mm from axis.

Print nema34_shaft_fit_coupon.stl first and check it with the real shaft AND
installed metal key. It should slide on with little play. If it binds, adjust
RADIAL_CLEARANCE / KEY_SIDE_CLEARANCE / KEY_TOP_CLEARANCE in the source.
A fit coupon checks fit only; it does not establish strength.

LEVER
Shaft axis to end-hole centre: 150.0. To extreme tip: 164.0.
Overall envelope: 186 x 44 x 26. Arm thickness: 16.0.
Hub diameter: 44.0. Hub height: 26.0. End hole: 6.5 diameter.
Solid tapered arm, no lightening holes. Bore has 0.5 mm circular lead-ins.

HARDWARE
One STEEL parallel key, 5 x 5 x 20.
One M5 x 16 socket set screw (flat point preferred against the key).
One standard full-height M5 hex nut (8 mm across flats, about 4 mm thick).
No hardware is part of the printable STL. Use the metal key even if a screw
alone appears to hold the unloaded lever.

ASSEMBLY
Seat the key in the motor groove. The hub groove is on the screw side (+Y).
Slide the lever on, leaving clearance from the motor face. The motor has a
32 mm shaft and a 25 mm long keyway: place the lever so the 20 mm key is fully
inside both grooves and the radial screw contacts its middle. The lever bore
is through, so the shaft need not protrude from the far side of the hub.
Drop the M5 nut into the top pocket, one hex corner pointing down. Insert the
M5 screw radially through the hub side and nut; tighten gently onto the steel
key. The nut provides the thread. The printed screw passage is clearance only.
Screw axis: 13 mm above lever underside. Check engagement before loading.

PRINT
Print with the broad arm face on the bed, raised hub upwards. Use a proven
functional-part profile with substantial walls and a solid hub/root region.
Clear the 5.6 mm radial passage and nut pocket if necessary. Printed strength
depends on material, layer bonding and process. This lever is not load rated
and has not been physically tested at 4.8 N m.

FOOD-SCALE TEST
At 4.8 N m with the lever horizontal and force vertical:
- End-hole centre (150 mm): about 3263 grams.
- Extreme tip (164 mm): about 2985 grams.
Use the actual horizontal shaft-to-contact distance, not overall part length.
Actual stall force depends on driver current and speed; 4.8 N m is the motor's
advertised HOLDING torque. Zero/tare initial contact load. Start in contact,
use low speed and low current initially, and keep hands outside the sweep.
The scale must have enough capacity for the applied load. Avoid impacts.
