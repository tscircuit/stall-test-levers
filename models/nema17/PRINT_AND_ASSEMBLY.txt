NEMA 17 STALL-TEST LEVER — all dimensions in mm

FITTING ASSUMPTION
StepperOnline brand and NEMA 17 frame size do not identify shaft dimensions.
This version assumes a 5.00 mm shaft, 4.50 mm flat-to-opposite-round distance.
The D socket is 5.30 mm diameter and 4.80 mm across D: 0.15 mm clearance per
surface. The flat faces toward the set screw. Shaft hole goes all the way through.
Print nema17_shaft_fit_coupon.stl first. It should slide on with little play. A loose
fit on a smaller shaft is not acceptable for a stall test; change the source
dimensions rather than relying on the screw to bridge that gap.

GEOMETRY
Shaft axis to end-hole centre: 150 mm. Shaft axis to very end: 162 mm.
Overall envelope approximately 180 x 36 x 22 mm (see geometry_check.json).
Arm: 12 mm thick, tapered from 36 mm root width to 24 mm at the tip.
Hub: 36 mm diameter x 22 mm tall; socket entry chamfers: 0.5 mm.
End attachment hole: 6.5 mm diameter.

HARDWARE AND ASSEMBLY
One M4 x 16 socket set screw and one full-height standard M4 hex nut
(7 mm across flats, about 3.2 mm thick). No hardware is included in the STL.
Drop the nut into the top slot with a hex corner pointing down. Its threaded
hole aligns with the radial screw passage. Insert the screw from the hub side
and tighten gently onto the shaft flat. The printed hole is clearance only.
The screw centre is 11 mm above the underside; position the lever along the
shaft so that the screw contacts the actual shaft flat. Leave clearance
between the lever and the stationary motor face. Do not force the fit.

PRINTING
The supplied orientation has the broad arm face on the bed, hub upwards.
Use your established strong functional-part profile, with substantial walls
and a solid hub/root region. Inspect the horizontal screw passage and clear
it to 4.5 mm if necessary. Clean the nut pocket until the nut seats fully.
Printing process, material, and layer bonding control strength. This geometry
has not been physically tested or structurally rated.

STALL TEST
Use a secured stop/load fixture, with hands outside the sweep. Test at low
speed and low current first; avoid impacts. At 150 mm perpendicular loading,
tip force in newtons = torque in N m / 0.150 (e.g. 3 N m gives 20 N).
This is a leverage calculation, not a safe-load rating. A longer lever reduces
required tip force but does not reduce the torque carried by the hub/root.

EDITING / REGENERATION
Edit SHAFT_DIAMETER, SHAFT_ACROSS_D, RADIAL_CLEARANCE, or LEVER_RADIUS at the
top of nema17_stall_lever.py. Tested using Python 3.12 and build123d 0.13.0.
With build123d installed, run: python nema17_stall_lever.py
The script exports STEP/STL files beside itself. Geometry validity and STL
mesh checks for the supplied exports are recorded in geometry_check.json.
